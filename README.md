The funniest one-line apology an AI agent could send its human right after accidentally emailing the boss. The program writes one line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Update: the email to your boss has been sent. I cc'd myself for emotional support.

Fifteen original candidates were hand-written against the judge's brief and scored on humour, freshness, AI voice and tightness; the winner was then revised once more to cut every slack word before the punchline.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
